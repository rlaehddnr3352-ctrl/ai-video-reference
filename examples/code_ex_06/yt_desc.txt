▶ 카드로 AI 영상 프롬프트 만드는 무료 사이트: https://ai-video-reference-kr.netlify.app/

영상 AI 없이, Claude에게 프롬프트를 주고 코드(HTML·Canvas·Three.js)로 그리게 만든 카페·음료 광고 2편이에요. 영상 속 브랜드·숫자는 모두 지어낸 예시예요.

① 음료가 차오르는 카페 신메뉴 소개
바꿔 쓰는 법: 카페 이름, 음료 3개의 이름·가격·층별 색만 바꾸면 돼요. 디저트라면 '잔 대신 접시 위 케이크가 층층이 쌓이게'처럼 바꿔 달라고 하세요.

프롬프트 전문 ①
Create an 11-second vertical (1080x1920) new-menu promo for a cafe as one HTML file with Canvas 2D. Expose window.render(t) that draws the frame at time t (seconds) deterministically, so it can be recorded frame by frame.

Cafe: [CAFE NAME]. Three drinks (name, price, 3 layer colors bottom→top): [DRINK 1], [DRINK 2], [DRINK 3]. Badge: "[NEW / 시즌 한정]".

Layout: one drink per screen, carousel style.
For each drink (~3.2s): a clear glass (trapezoid outline, lid and straw) sits center; its liquid layers pour in bottom to top with a wobbling surface line (sine wave) and a few rising bubbles; the background color shifts to match the drink; the name rises in under the glass and the price slides in on a rounded tag; small ingredient dots float around.
Between drinks, the whole layout slides left like a card carousel (0.5s ease-in-out) with page dots at the bottom.
End (9.8–11s): the three glasses line up side by side, the cafe name and "[OPEN INFO]" appear; fade out.

Flat illustration style, pastel tones, Noto Sans KR.

② 잔이 층층이 차오르는 음료 레시피
바꿔 쓰는 법: 음료 이름과 층마다 재료·양·색만 바꾸면 돼요. 칵테일이면 술 이름과 양을 넣으면 되고, 층이 4개면 '4층으로'라고 적어 주세요.

프롬프트 전문 ②
Create a 12-second vertical (1080x1920) layered drink recipe animation as one HTML file with Canvas 2D. Expose window.render(t) that draws the frame at time t (seconds) deterministically, so it can be recorded frame by frame.

Drink: [DRINK NAME]. Layers bottom → top (ingredient, amount, color): [딸기청 40ml #e63946], [우유 200ml #fdf6ec], [에스프레소 1샷 #6f4518]. Garnish: [GARNISH].

A tall clear glass (slightly tapered, glossy highlight stripe) stands center on a pastel background.
0–1s: drink name rises in at the top; the empty glass fades in.
1–8s: each layer is poured in turn (~2.2s): a stream falls from above into the glass (wavy line), the layer rises with a gently sloshing top surface (sine wave that calms down), and slightly mixes into the layer below at the boundary (soft gradient). As each layer finishes, a leader line draws out to the side and a label card shows the ingredient and amount.
8–9.5s: ice cubes drop in and bob; the garnish pops on the rim.
9.5–12s: a small "총 [TOTAL]ml · [TIME]분" tag appears; fade out.

Bright, clean recipe-card style, Noto Sans KR + Inter for numbers.

만든 영상을 mp4로 뽑을 땐 Claude에게 "render(t)로 초당 30장 캡처해서 mp4로 합쳐 줘"라고 하면 돼요.
