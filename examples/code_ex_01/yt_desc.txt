▶ 카드로 AI 영상 프롬프트 만드는 무료 사이트: https://ai-video-reference-kr.netlify.app/

영상 AI 없이, Claude에게 프롬프트를 주고 코드(HTML·Canvas·Three.js)로 그리게 만든 할인 이벤트 광고 2편이에요. 영상 속 브랜드·숫자는 모두 지어낸 예시예요.

① 가격이 뚝 떨어지는 마감 할인 카운트다운
바꿔 쓰는 법: 상품명, 정가·할인가·할인율, 마감 문구만 바꾸면 돼요. 타이머 시작 시간을 실제 남은 시간으로 바꾸고 싶으면 '03:00:00부터'처럼 적어 주세요.

프롬프트 전문 ①
Create a 10-second vertical (1080x1920) flash-sale countdown ad as one HTML file with Canvas 2D. Expose window.render(t) that draws the frame at time t (seconds) deterministically, so it can be recorded frame by frame.

Product: [PRODUCT NAME]. Regular price: [39,000원]. Sale price: [19,900원]. Discount badge: [-49%]. Deadline text: "[오늘 밤 12시 마감]".
Palette: black, electric yellow #ffe14d, hot red.

0–1s: yellow diagonal stripes sweep across a black screen; "TIME SALE" slams in.
1–4s: a big seven-segment-style digital timer (draw segments as rounded bars) counts down quickly from 02:59:59 with the seconds blurring; a progress bar under it drains.
3–5.5s: the product card slides in; the regular price is shown, then a red strike line draws across it from left to right.
5.5–7s: the sale price drops from above and lands with a squash-and-stretch bounce and a screen shake of 8px; the discount badge stamps in at an angle with a starburst behind it.
7–10s: a "[오늘만]" pill blinks twice per second, the deadline text sits under the price; fade out.

Bold Korean type (Noto Sans KR Black), thick shapes, high contrast.

② 슬롯머신으로 뽑는 오늘의 혜택
바꿔 쓰는 법: [EVENT NAME], 릴에 들어갈 혜택 6개, 당첨 혜택, 마감 문구만 바꾸면 우리 가게 이벤트 영상이 돼요. 레버 대신 '버튼을 누르는 손가락'으로 바꿔 달라고 해도 됩니다.

프롬프트 전문 ②
Create an 11-second vertical (1080x1920) slot-machine promo animation as one HTML file with Canvas 2D. window.render(t) draws frame t deterministically.

Event: "[EVENT NAME]" for [BRAND]. Reel symbols (text labels drawn on tiles): [1+1], [무료배송], [10%], [★], [20%], [사은품]. Winning symbol: [WINNING BENEFIT].

Machine: a chunky rounded cabinet in [BRAND COLOR] with a marquee of bulbs around the top sign that chase in sequence, three reel windows with glass highlights, and a lever on the right with a red ball.
0–1.5s: the cabinet rises into frame with a bounce; marquee reads "[EVENT NAME]".
1.5–2.2s: the lever is pulled down and springs back.
2–6.8s: the three reels spin with motion blur (draw stretched, semi-transparent tiles), then stop one by one at 5.0s, 5.7s and 6.6s with a slight overshoot and settle; the last reel slows down suspensefully.
6.8–9s: all three show the winning symbol — the bulbs flash in unison, a winning line glows across the reels, confetti bursts, and a banner "[WIN MESSAGE]" drops in.
9–11s: CTA "[CTA — e.g. 오늘 자정까지]" pulses below, then fade out.

Style: playful casino-arcade, bold Korean type (Noto Sans KR Black), dark purple background with sparkles.
